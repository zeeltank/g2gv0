# 05 Discovery-call kit (draft; nothing here has been sent)

Why: the CRM holds no real principal or trustee language (see 01). Until real calls exist, every school scene in the deck stays "illustrative, to validate with you", and no sentence may say "leaders tell us ...". I cannot arrange or place a call and I never send messages; a person on your side books it and sends the invitation.

## Rules
1. Real principal, trustee, director or academic head only. 5 to 10 calls across different board types and sizes; the first one is enough to start.
2. Recorded only after a clear yes, said on the recording and confirmed in writing.
3. **Never invent or paraphrase into a quote.** If a call does not happen, the deck stays illustrative. Nothing is "recalled from memory".
4. No customer or school is named in the deck without explicit written permission (separate from the recording yes).
5. A phrase may be presented as "school leaders say" only after at least 3 independent leaders said it (my proposal; you can change the number). Fewer than that: keep "illustrative".
6. No pitching, no product names, no price. The call is about their world.

## Invitation text (draft, to be sent by you or your team, not by me)
> Hello [name], I am [your name] from ScholarClone. We are building tools for school leaders and want to understand how schools actually work before we show anything. Could you give me 20 minutes to hear how you find out what needs attention in your school? It is a conversation, not a sales call, and I will not show a product. With your permission I would record it so I can listen again. You can stop the recording at any time. Would [day] or [day] suit you?

## Consent (read aloud at the start, then confirm in writing)
> Before we start: with your permission I would like to record this call so I can listen again and take accurate notes. The recording is kept private by our team. I will not use your name, your school's name or your exact words anywhere outside the team unless you tell me in writing that I may. You can ask me to stop recording or delete it at any time. Is that all right?

## 20-minute guide (ask about what happened, not what they would do)
| Minutes | Ask | Listen for |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Consent; their role and the type and size of school | board, size band, role |
| 2 to 8 | "Tell me about the last time you found out late that a student needed help." "Walk me through how you decide where to look first on a normal morning." | which sources, how late, who tells whom |
| 8 to 13 | "What do you do with a report once you have it?" "How do you know whether a teacher's training changed anything?" "Last month, how did fee follow-up actually go?" | what happens after the report; workarounds; tools mentioned |
| 13 to 17 | Show only the six-step line from slide 4 (no product): "Where does your school's information stop?" Then show slide 1 only: "What do you think your school might not be seeing?" | their own words for the problem; whether the hook lands |
| 17 to 20 | "Who else should I talk to?" "May I quote anything you said, without your name?" Thank them. | introductions; written quote permission |

## What the calls change
Each theme that three or more leaders raise can become a "what leaders tell us" line; contradictions with a slide mean the slide is rewritten; the slide-1 reactions decide whether the hook or one of the three alternative openers goes first. Recordings and transcripts are stored privately on D: (not in the repo) and handled under your privacy policy; the repo only ever holds anonymised phrases.
