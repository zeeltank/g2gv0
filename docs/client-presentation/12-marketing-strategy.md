# 12 Marketing strategy for the explainer clips (one page)

Date 2026-11-24. Built from the style study in `11-explainer-style-notes.md` and held to the rules in `01-promoter-language-and-banned-claims.md`. Nothing here is a result; there is no performance data yet. Tags: **verified** (checked this session), **inferred** (my reasoning, to be tested).

## The idea
Each reference Short makes **one point in the first two seconds and proves it with a few big cards**. ScholarClone's clips do the same, in the language of a school leader's own questions, and they never claim more than the code does.

| Clip | The question it opens with (2 s) | What it proves in the next 8 s | Claims it rests on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insight | What needs attention today? | Three signals become one case; a person approves or rejects | C-INS-CASE, C-INS-GATE |
| Core | Fees, attendance and marks. One system? | Three everyday jobs go from working to done | C-CORE-FEE, -ATT, -EXM |
| Learn | Which concepts is this student weak in? | Weak, moderate, strong; a plan; one next action | C-LRN-DIAG, -PLAN, -PRAC, -NEXT |
| CCB | Where does a teacher need development? | Ratings against a required level; the gap; a plan with actions | C-CCB-RATE, -GAP, -PLAN |
| Compass | What do a student's interests say about careers? | Interests compared with aspiration; "not enough evidence yet" is shown, not hidden | C-CMP-PROF, -ALIGN |

Why this order (inferred): principals and trustees open with "what needs attention" and "one system"; the teacher and student questions come after trust exists. Compass goes last because its honest "not enough evidence yet" is a talking point, not an opener.

## Rules for every clip and every post
1. **One idea, three or four cards, one outcome sentence of at most 14 words.** The checker enforces the word limits.
2. **Captions are burned in** (most people watch muted) and come from `clips.json`, so the same banned-word and claim checks cover them.
3. **Say what it is.** "Illustrative school data" stays on screen. The clips are motion graphics, not recordings; the post text must not call them a "demo of the live screens" or "a recording".
4. **No numbers that sound like results**: no percentages, savings, speed-ups, prices, seat counts or dates (all enforced by the checker for clip text; apply the same to post text by hand).
5. **No customer named** without written permission; **no testimonial**; **no "free", "guaranteed", "proven"**; do not describe Insight as predicting or finding students early; do not say "one login" or "AI questions are reviewed".
6. **The call to action is only the approved invitation**: "We would like to talk with you about the Founding 25 Pilot Programme." A post may add a way to reply (a name and an address from the owner); it may not add a price, a trial, a discount, a date or a number of seats.

## Formats and where each goes
| Format | Files | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16, 15 s, closing card with the product name | `public/client-presentation/clips/<clip>-v-hidden.mp4` + poster | LinkedIn, WhatsApp status and broadcast, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| 16:9, 15 s | `<clip>-h-hidden.mp4` + poster | Follow-up e-mail, website, the PowerPoint copy |
| Live in the deck | slide 16 "See it work" and the "Watch the explainer" tile on slides 7, 8, 10, 11, 12 | Meetings: crisp at any screen size, no video weight |

## A first five weeks (inferred; change after the first reads)
One clip a week, in the order above, each as: the 9:16 video, a two-line post that repeats the opening question, and the invitation sentence as the last line. A short reply to every comment that names the product's real limit when asked (for example, "Insight shows what links to what; it does not say what caused it").

## How we will know it works (not measured yet)
- **Two-second test:** show the first two seconds to five school leaders who have not seen ScholarClone; each says what the clip is about. Pass = at least four say it in their own words. Not done.
- **Watch-through and replies** on the posts: record, per clip, how many watched to the end and how many replied. Replies from a school leader matter more than views. No target is set until there is a first reading.
- **Module owner sign-off** per clip (`confirmed_by_module_owner` in `clips-register.json`). Not done.

## Not yet true, so not claimed
Real screen recordings of the product; any measured result for a school; a customer story; the effect of the clips on enquiries.
